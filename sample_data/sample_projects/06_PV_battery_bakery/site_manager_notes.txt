Project: Rooftop PV with battery and blackout backup – Pekárna Javor (bakery), Zlín region
Company: ENERGO NWT
Public reference: https://energo.nwt.cz/project/fotovoltaicka-elektrarna-pekarna-javor/

PV power 86.42 kWp, battery 233 kWh, max. charge/discharge power 90 kVA.
Backup function: during a grid outage part of the PV keeps running, so the oven conveyor keeps moving
and products don't burn; limited operation possible for several days.
Bakery is known in the region (e.g. traditional wedding pastries).
