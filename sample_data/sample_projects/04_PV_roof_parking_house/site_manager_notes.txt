Project: Rooftop PV on the parking house of a shopping centre – OC Futurum, Brno
Company: AGRO NWT
Public reference: https://agro.nwt.cz/reference/futurum-brno

Total power 998.8 kWp: 1,816 panels x 550 Wp, 908 optimizers.
Custom-designed ballasted mounting structure, east–west orientation (no anchoring into the roof deck).
All technology placed under a canopy on the roof.
Connected to the shopping centre's main switchboard.
Logistics: work only outside shopping hours near the ramps; crane slots booked with centre management.
